# OpenXcom3D 1.06

*Stand on the battlescape of* UFO: Enemy Unknown *and look around through a soldier's eyes. Now version 1.06: four more of a veteran player's reports, and the big mods' spray weapons, energy costs and drones made at home in 3D.*

> **New here? ▶ [How to play these downloads](https://b0realis.github.io/OpenXcom3D/play.html)**, on Linux, Windows, the Steam Deck or macOS, and where your UFO and TFTD data goes. Rather compile it? **⚙ [The build guide](https://b0realis.github.io/OpenXcom3D/)**.

![Terror From The Deep: inside the Triton, the squad round, a Vibro Blade in hand](v1.06/tftd_triton_squad.png)

Patches 137–158, from four play reports on 1.05, each discussed, built and tested on its own: a robust step per patch (with new test mods for spray weapons, action costs, heat vision and an X-Com Files scout drone stand-in), then the full suite (AddressSanitizer, ThreadSanitizer) on the final code. Most of it moves decisions from the facing tile to the crosshair: melee and medi-kits now resolve their target from the voxel and unit pictures under it, not from the soldier's eighth. The spray burst reuses OXCE's own targeting (`BattleAction::sprayTargeting` through `primaryAction`, which reads Ctrl and Shift itself), the cost line reads `BattleActionCost` and the game's `haveTU` rules, and heat vision applies the armor's `heatVision` share to the drawn smoke as the sight trace already did to the smoke's cost.

Everything still goes through the original game: time units, energy, accuracy, reaction fire, line of sight, doors and morale. Everything new is a setting or the game's own rule.

## New in 1.06

### Everything by the crosshair

- **Melee** strikes the wall, door or object under the crosshair on the soldier's own tile or any next to it, diagonally and a level up or down too: on stairs, the walls of both floors ahead (with *Melee hits walls and downed*, or a mod's terrain melee).
- **Medi-kits** treat whoever is under the crosshair on a tile beside you, turning to face them first (paid with *Turning costs TU*), or the body at your feet; a unit farther off is out of range, and a body elsewhere asks you to stand over it.
- **Every cost of an action** on a third line under the hit chance (*Expanded crosshair info*): energy, health, stun, morale and mana, a cost the soldier can't pay in red. The original games' weapons cost only time units; the big mods' often more.

![UFO: a medi-kit aimed at the teammate beside the soldier, "USE: Fred Katundu"](v1.06/ufo_medikit_teammate.png)

### Spray bursts

The big mods' miniguns and machine guns (X-Com Files', XPiratez's) sweep their burst between points. In 3D as on the map: with the auto shot, **Ctrl+Shift and a click** marks the first point, each click the next, and the last fires; the right button takes one back. Each point stands in the view as a light green pole with a mini target where the rounds will go, its number beside it, so a point far off is seen too.

### Flying with the view

With flight on, **W looking up flies straight up** and looking down comes down, as on a grav lift, level by level while held. Arrows beside the compass show where W can climb from here, the one it takes now lit.

![UFO: flying over a farm, looking down, an enemy spotted: the climb arrow by the compass lit](v1.06/ufo_flight_climb_arrow.png)

### Seeing

- **Heat vision thins the smoke you see:** a scout drone's 50% heat vision sees through half the smoke drawn, as it already did by the game's sight rules.
- **Shots into the sky just vanish:** no more sparks and dust where a shot leaves the map.
- **Cydonia's lift bars stand round the lift like a cage**, shown with *Parts without shape: Lifts* too; and **Ghost opacity** (10 to 100%) sets how solid the parts without shape look.
- **Items on stairs** are drawn over the steps they lie on instead of half inside them.

### And more

- **The soldier's rank before the name** on the panel, the cards and the soldier cam: a mod's own tiny ranks (XPiratez), or the game's own badge made small.
- **Numpad diagonals:** 8 and 4 pressed together step diagonally, as W and A do.
- **A 2×2 unit is no longer blinded** standing in a two-tile door: its eye steps out of the door's frame.
- **A weapon drawn on a slant lies along the gun** (XPiratez's quarterstaff stood in the air), and the soldier's rank badge sits level with the name.

![Terror From The Deep: an island, a Gill Man in the crosshair](v1.06/tftd_island_gill_man.png)

## Download

You need your own copy of *UFO: Enemy Unknown / X-COM: UFO Defense* and/or *X-COM: Terror From The Deep*. Nothing of it is included.

- **`OpenXcom3D-1.06-x86_64.AppImage`**: one file. Make it executable and run it.
- **`OpenXcom3D-1.06-linux-x86_64.tar.gz`**: unpack it anywhere and run `./play.sh`.
- **`OpenXcom3D-1.06-windows-x86_64.zip`**: for 64-bit Windows 10 and 11. Unpack it somewhere of yours (Documents, the Desktop, `C:\Games`; not Program Files) and double-click `play.bat`. The game isn't signed, so Windows may warn the first time: *More info*, then *Run anyway*.

The Linux ones run on any 64-bit Linux from 2021 on (Ubuntu 22.04 and newer, Debian 12, Fedora, Arch, the Steam Deck). All three find your games in your Steam library by themselves. Every key, setting and step is in the [manual](../../OpenXcom3D_manual.md). A first macOS build, untested on a real Mac, comes from the automatic builds ([how](../../OpenXcom3D_manual.md#option-a-on-macos-a-first-untested-build)).
