# OpenXcom3D 1.12

*UFO: Enemy Unknown, through a soldier's eyes. Now it knows who's hurt, who's hidden and how far you can see.*

## New

- **View distance** up to 200 tiles, and **Unseen terrain** as mist, fog or in plain sight.
- **Fog of war in 3D**, a setting: unexplored floor stays dark, as on the map.
- **Wall paint**: a wall's face the map never shows takes the paint of the side it faces: a house from behind is its outside.
- **Enemy markers in the map buttons' colours**: red seen, green seen by the squad, yellow hidden (smoke, dark, camouflage) but in a clear line of fire.
- **Wounded markers**: our own wounded in blue, those about to pass out in purple, numbered as their buttons.
- **Damage numbers**, a setting: health red, armour blue, stun purple, in 3D and on the map.
- **Ambient sounds**: rain, wind and nearby fires, made by the game, muffled under a roof.
- **Camera artifacts**: the helmet cameras' static as it is, lighter, or none.
- **Full sprite hitboxes, exact**: a unit is hit as drawn, walking or flying, bows and other arcs too; *Hitboxes* outlines what the shots test.
- **Sprint en passant**: a sprint walks on past enemies coming into sight; any walk stops when shot at, as on the map.

## Fixed

- A laser up close leaves the gun, with its flash and kick.
- Bows and other arcing shots at big units.
- A guided missile's waypoints capped in 3D, as on the map.
- A built-in weapon with both hands full.
- A unit straight below no longer spins when shot.
- A sidestep with too few time units no longer jitters.
- Downed unit icons only for the body under the crosshair.
- *Pixel size* goes round.
- Seven setting values too wide for their column renamed; *Soldier camera preview* no longer in capitals.
- Smoke: *Original* is now the cheaper look, the one for a slow machine.
- The build guide's Windows route with MSYS2's new SDL.

## Download

Your own *UFO* and/or *TFTD* needed.

- **`OpenXcom3D-1.12-x86_64.AppImage`**: make it executable, run it.
- **`OpenXcom3D-1.12-linux-x86_64.tar.gz`**: unpack, run `./play.sh`.
- **`OpenXcom3D-1.12-windows-x86_64.zip`**: unpack (not in Program Files), run `play.bat`.
- **`OpenXcom3D-1.12-macos.zip`** (Apple silicon and Intel, macOS 11 and newer): made by the automatic build, not tried on a Mac yet.

SHA256:
